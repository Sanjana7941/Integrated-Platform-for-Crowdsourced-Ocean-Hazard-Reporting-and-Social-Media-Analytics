# 🌊 Integrated Platform for Crowdsourced Ocean Hazard Reporting and Social Media Analytics

> **Empowering coastal communities with AI-driven ocean hazard monitoring, real-time reporting, and social media intelligence.**

---

## 📌 Overview

The **Integrated Platform for Crowdsourced Ocean Hazard Reporting and Social Media Analytics** is an intelligent web-based solution designed to improve coastal safety by combining **crowdsourced hazard reporting**, **AI-powered analytics**, and **social media monitoring**.

The platform enables citizens, fishermen, tourists, and authorities to report ocean-related hazards in real time while simultaneously analyzing public social media posts to detect emerging risks, helping emergency teams respond faster and make informed decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Coastal regions often face hazards such as:

* 🌊 High tides and storm surges
* 🌀 Cyclones and rough sea conditions
* 🛢️ Oil spills and water pollution
* 🪼 Jellyfish blooms
* 🚢 Marine accidents
* 🌧️ Coastal flooding

Traditional reporting methods are slow and depend heavily on manual communication, delaying emergency response.

---

## 💡 Solution

This platform creates a centralized system where:

* Citizens can instantly report ocean hazards.
* AI validates and categorizes hazard reports.
* Social media posts are analyzed to identify trending incidents.
* Authorities receive real-time alerts.
* Interactive dashboards provide actionable insights.

## 📊 Workflow

1. User reports an ocean hazard.
2. Location and media are uploaded.
3. AI analyzes the report.
4. Social media data is monitored simultaneously.
5. Hazard severity is determined.
6. Authorities receive notifications.
7. Dashboard updates in real time.
8. Response teams take necessary action.
